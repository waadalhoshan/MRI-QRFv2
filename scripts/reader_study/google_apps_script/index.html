MRI Image Readability Assessment

Rater Information & Consent

Background

MRI images can be affected by image-quality degradation that may reduce visual clarity and make anatomical interpretation more difficult.

Purpose of this task

You are being asked to provide your independent clinical judgment on the readability of a set of structural MRI images.

What you will be asked to do

  • You will view 160 MRI images , one at a time.
  • For each image, rate its readability using one of three categories.
  • Readable — anatomical structures can be interpreted with adequate confidence.
  • Borderline — image quality is degraded, but the main anatomical structures remain interpretable with reduced confidence.
  • Unreadable — image degradation substantially prevents reliable anatomical interpretation.

Please assess image readability only . You are not being asked to diagnose or stage disease.

Blinding

The images are identified only by anonymous image IDs. Information about the source dataset, diagnostic class, image-quality condition, and degradation severity is not shown during rating.

Data source and privacy

The images are derived from publicly available, de-identified research MRI data. No patient names or protected health information are displayed.

Participation

Participation is voluntary. Progress is saved to the study response sheet after every rating. If you leave and return later, enter the same rater code to continue from your first unrated image.
